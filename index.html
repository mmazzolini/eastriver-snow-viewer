About the data

Snow depth from the MuSA data-assimilation framework (FSM2 snow model, iterative ensemble smoother, 100 members) driven by TopoPyScale-downscaled ERA5. ICESat-2 snow depth at 400 cells is assimilated and spread to 5400 model cells with a Gaussian-process (GPE) or Gaspari–Cohn (GCE) prior correlation. The ensembles are mapped to the 50 m grid with Gaussian-process regression and compared with Airborne Snow Observatory (ASO) lidar on 1 April 2023 and 3 April 2024.

Profile values follow the paper figure: each step is the mean over the profile cells that have model values and ASO; bands are the mean of the per-cell percentiles; IC2 cells show the ensemble of the observed cell. A segment shows, for each member, the mean over all the profile cells between its two lines (c) or over the IC2 cells between them (f); the IC2 likelihood of a segment is that of the mean observation with independent errors, as in the assimilation. The website stores every member rounded to the centimetre, so the values shown are within 5 mm of the full-precision files in the data record.

Gridded products for comparison, on their own grid cells (each 50 m cell takes the value of the product cell around it): UA 800 m (University of Arizona daily snow depth, SNOTEL and COOP stations assimilated into a PRISM-driven snow model; Broxton et al. 2024), SNODAS (NOAA NOHRSC, 1 km, 06 UTC snapshot, model updated daily with observations; NSIDC G02158) and WLDAS (NASA, Noah-MP driven by NLDAS-2, 0.01°, no snow data assimilation; 2023 only, as the record ends in January 2024). Their CRPS in the scores table is the mean absolute error, as they are single values.
